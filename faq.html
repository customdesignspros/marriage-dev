Answers to Common Questions

General Questions

What is MarriageDisputes.com?

MarriageDisputes.com is an online marketplace designed to connect individuals and families with family-law attorneys, mediators, and other dispute-resolution professionals.

Does MarriageDisputes.com provide legal advice?

No. MarriageDisputes.com is a marketplace and information platform. Legal services are provided independently by professionals you choose to contact. No attorney-client relationship is created by using this website.

Are the professionals on the platform attorneys?

Some professionals may be attorneys, while others may be mediators or other qualified professionals. Review each professional's profile and credentials before making contact.

How do I find a professional?

Start by selecting the type of service you need and review professionals offering that service. You can compare professionals based on experience, practice areas, services, location, and other profile information.

Does MarriageDisputes.com guarantee results?

No. Legal and family disputes depend on individual circumstances, applicable law, negotiations, and decisions by courts or other authorities. No outcome is guaranteed.

Can I use MarriageDisputes.com if I am only considering divorce?

Yes. You can explore professionals and learn about potential options before deciding what to do. The platform is designed to help you understand your options early in the process.

About Mediation

What is divorce mediation?

Divorce mediation is a process in which a neutral mediator helps divorcing spouses communicate and attempt to reach agreements concerning issues arising from their divorce, such as property division, child custody, support, and parenting schedules.

Is a mediator my attorney?

Generally, a mediator is neutral and does not represent one spouse against the other. You may choose to have your own attorney review agreements or advise you separately.

Is mediation less expensive than litigation?

It can be, depending on the circumstances, but costs vary based on the professionals involved, complexity of the dispute, and number of sessions required. Discuss fees directly with any professional you contact.

Can mediation work if we disagree?

Yes. Mediation is specifically designed to help parties address disagreements. However, it may not be appropriate for every situation. A qualified professional can advise you on whether mediation may be suitable.

What if mediation doesn't work?

You may have other options, including attorney negotiation or litigation. A qualified professional can explain the options available to you in your specific circumstances.

Child Custody & Parenting

What is child custody?

Child custody generally involves legal decision-making and/or physical care arrangements for children. Terminology and legal standards vary by jurisdiction.

Can custody be resolved through mediation?

In many situations, parents can use mediation to negotiate parenting arrangements, subject to applicable law and court requirements. A mediator or family-law attorney can advise you on available options.

What is a parenting plan?

A parenting plan sets out how parents will share parenting responsibilities and time with their children, including schedules, holidays, decision-making authority, and other arrangements.

Costs & Fees

How much does a divorce attorney cost?

Fees vary significantly depending on location, professional experience, complexity, and billing structure. Discuss fees directly with the professional before retaining them.

How much does mediation cost?

Mediation fees vary depending on the mediator, location, complexity, number of sessions, and other factors. Contact professionals directly through the marketplace to discuss their fees.

Is my information confidential?

Do not assume information submitted through a marketplace is protected by attorney-client privilege. Review the site's Privacy Policy and understand that an attorney-client relationship generally is not created merely by submitting information through the platform.

For Attorneys & Mediators

Can I join MarriageDisputes.com as a professional?

Professionals who meet the platform's requirements may apply to create a professional profile. This includes family-law attorneys, divorce attorneys, mediators, collaborative divorce professionals, and other qualified dispute-resolution providers.

Does MarriageDisputes.com guarantee clients?

No. Marketplace participation does not guarantee leads, clients, engagements, or revenue. The platform provides a way for you to be discovered by individuals seeking your services.
